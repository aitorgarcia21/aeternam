## Gkioni et al. 2025 — rapamycine, tramétinib, les deux

Gkioni L. et al., « The geroprotectors trametinib and rapamycin combine additively to extend
mouse healthspan and lifespan », *Nature Aging* 2025;5:1249-1265,
doi:10.1038/s43587-025-00876-4 (PMC12270913). Institut Max-Planck de biologie du
vieillissement, Cologne. Souris C3B6F1.

- Tramétinib seul : « in female mice with a median lifespan extension of 7.2% (P = 0.0248,
  log-rank test) » ; « in male mice with an increase in median lifespan of 10.2% ».
- Rapamycine seule : « an increase in median and maximum lifespan of 17.4% and 16.5%,
  respectively, in female mice (Fig. 2b) and 16.6% and 18.3%, respectively, in male mice ».
- Les deux : « median and maximum lifespan increased by 34.9% and 32.4%, respectively, in female
  mice (Fig. 2b) and by 27.4% and 26.1%, respectively, in male mice ».
- Rapports de risque, femelles : « the hazard ratio of trametinib alone compared to control
  (P = 0.0383) was 0.6926 (0.4886–0.9804) and 0.4478 (0.3002–0.6641) in the presence of
  rapamycin » ; « Rapamycin alone had a hazard ratio of 0.3284 (0.2243–0.4784) compared to
  controls (P < 0.0001) and 0.2123 (0.1380–0.3234) in the presence of trametinib ».
- Rapports de risque, mâles : « trametinib alone had a hazard ratio of 0.5257 (0.3845–0.7169)
  versus controls (P < 0.0001) and 0.5814 (0.4127–0.8154) in the presence of rapamycin » ;
  « Rapamycin on its own had a hazard ratio of 0.4623 (0.3376–0.6316) compared to controls
  (P < 0.0001) and 0.5113 (0.3611–0.7208) in the presence of trametinib ».
- Conclusion des auteurs : « There was, thus, no indication of a positive or negative
  interaction between drug treatments in either sex. »
- Effectifs (légende de la figure 2) : « control ♂ n = 121, ♀ n = 100; rapamycin ♂ n = 120,
  ♀ n = 98, trametinib ♂ n = 121, ♀ n = 98; combined ♂ n = 120, ♀ n = 98 ». Courbes regroupant
  les cohortes de longévité, de prélèvement de tissus et de phénotypage ; traitements menés en
  même temps que les témoins.
- Différence entre sexes signalée par les auteurs : « combined treatment with rapamycin and
  trametinib increased survival more in female mice than in male mice (sex–treatment
  interaction P = 0.0218, Cox proportional hazard) ».

## Strong et al. 2022 — rapamycine et acarbose

Strong R. et al., *Aging Cell* 2022;21:e13724, doi:10.1111/acel.13724 (PMC9741502). Programme
de tests d'interventions du National Institute on Aging : souris UM-HET3, trois sites.

- « This combination of agents produced a 28% increase in median lifespan in females
  (p < 0.0001) and a 34% increase in males (p < 0.0001) for data pooled across sites ».
- Sur deux sites seulement : « median increases for the (TJL + UM) data are 22% for females and
  29% for males ».
- Rapamycine seule, cohortes antérieures (tableau 5) : mâles 10 % (2006) et 5 % (2009) ;
  femelles 18 % et 18 %. Ce ne sont pas les mêmes souris ni les mêmes années : comparaison
  descriptive, pas un test.

## Harrison et al. 2014 — acarbose seul

Harrison D. E. et al., *Aging Cell* 2014;13:273-82, doi:10.1111/acel.12170 (résumé).
« Acarbose increased male median lifespan by 22% (P < 0.0001), but increased female median
lifespan by only 5% (P = 0.01). »
